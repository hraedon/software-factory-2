## On the project

The project is in a mature Phase 5 state. The pipeline has been exercised across 38 golden runs with progressively improving lock rates (95% in GR-034). The breadcrumb corpus (190 resolved, 3 open, 20 RFCs, 9 defect classes) is well-organized and demonstrates real learning from v1 mistakes.

The most striking thing is how many of the open items are "Phase 3" blockers — fleet integration, multi-channel scaling, data-driven placement. The current architecture works well at 2-3 channels but the design anticipates a much larger fleet. RFC-034 (model identity in telemetry) and RFC-035 (placement layer) are genuine Phase 3 blockers that need real implementation, not just filing.

The gate.py file (1400+ lines) is a god module. RFC-036 proposes splitting it into a package, and after working on BC-188 inside `evaluate_integration`, I'm convinced this split is overdue. Every gate change touches the same file regardless of gate kind (deterministic, subprocess, model-mediated, integration). The integration assembler alone is complex enough for its own module.
