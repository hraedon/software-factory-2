## On the project

The factory's architecture is holding up well under Phase 5 expansion. The 7-module decomposition plus the new StageHandoff topology in config.py cleanly accommodates new work-item types and roles without structural changes to runner, gate, or scheduler. The `GateResult.custom_fields` extension (added for BC-145) proved to be the right pivot point — it lets gate results carry structured findings through gate_process into substrate custom_fields, which the runner then reads back into PromptContext.

One tension: the `evaluate_integration()` gate is intentionally skeletal. It runs import resolution on a temp directory but skips mypy and pytest integration-gate variants because those require a real venv with assembled dependencies. The plan calls for `integration_mypy` and `integration_pytest` gates (bringing the deterministic total to 16 of 18), but those need per-DAG venv construction which doesn't exist yet. This is deferred risk.

## On the work done

BC-145 was the most delicate change. The `ReviewFinding` dataclass, structured findings schema, and `review_malformed` vs `review_found_defect` diagnostic taxonomy are clean. The router dispatch correctly routes `review_found_defect` to upstream revision (not escalation) while keeping `review_malformed` in the escalatable set. The `_ESCALATABLE_KINDS` management required care: `CROSS_FAMILY_REVIEW` (legacy string-diagnostic from Phase 4) stays escalatable because it lacks structured findings, while `REVIEW_FOUND_DEFECT` (new Phase 5 structured-diagnostic) does not.

The capability probe for `outcome_verifier` was straightforward and produced strong results — K2, DeepSeek, and GLM all scored 12/12 on the flawed-assembly test. This gives confidence in the prompt template. Less confidence: the `integrator` prompt is unproven in a real pipeline run. The instruction "do not modify signatures, only fix imports" may be too subtle for models.
