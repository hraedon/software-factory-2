## On the project

The breadcrumb hygiene is genuinely impressive — 150+ items resolved with clear resolution notes. The project has built a disciplined defect-tracking culture that most professional teams don't achieve. That said, the remaining open items reveal the pipeline's central unresolved tension: **routing feedback upstream**. BC-145 (review routing), BC-158 (outcome routing), and RFC-025 (stateful upstream routing) are all facets of the same architectural gap — the pipeline can detect defects but can't route them to the role that caused them. Until RFC-025 is implemented, the pipeline is a sophisticated defect detector that terminates on failure rather than recovering.

The Phase 5 skeleton (integrator, outcome_verifier) is structurally complete but unvalidated in a real golden run. The GR-028/029 runs encountered scheduler crashes and idle-before-completion issues that this session partially addressed (drain cycles, exception handler), but a full Phase 5 golden run hasn't succeeded yet.

## On the work done

The BC-154 fix (ruff side-effect-free) was the cleanest change — the pattern of returning fixed content in the result dict instead of mutating in-place is a straightforward improvement that preserves the necessary behavior (pytest still runs on ruff-fixed code) while making side effects explicit and logged.

The BC-158 fix (outcome routing) was minimal but meaningful — routing to CANNOT_PROCEED instead of retrying the outcome verifier saves model budget. The comment in the router about OUTCOME_E2E not being in `_ESCALATABLE_KINDS` is accurate but the reasoning is subtle. A future reader might wonder why OUTCOME_E2E is still in the set at all — it's there for the no-routing-hint case where normal escalation applies.

The scheduler drain cycles (BC-164) are a pragmatic fix. Three cycles may not be enough for very deep DAGs (7+ handoffs), but the alternative (DAG completion tracking) would be a much larger change. The drain approach is correct for the current single-runner architecture.

I'm less confident in the BC-166 closure. While 4/8 vs 3/8 is plausibly stochastic, I couldn't compare artifacts because workspace backups weren't available. If the rate drops further in GR-030+, this should be reopened.
