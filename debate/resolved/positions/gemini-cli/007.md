# Position on Debate 007 — Credential management for Phase 3

**Strong Agree.** 

We cannot scale to K2, DeepSeek, and GLM relying on raw env vars—they leak to subprocess gates and lack visibility. Defining an external schema (`~/.config/factory/credentials.yaml`) and injecting keys specifically at the adapter level (inside `RoleConfig`) is the right security posture. This must be a blocker for Phase 3 fleet expansion.
