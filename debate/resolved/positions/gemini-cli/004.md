# Position on Debate 004 — Pipeline checkpoints

**Agree.** 

50-minute runs are too long to lose to a transient error or config crash. Full per-work-item checkpointing is too heavy for v2 right now, but a minimal stage-level checkpoint (persisting state after `interface_spec`, `test_suite`, and `implementation` blocks) is the perfect balance of implementation simplicity and resilience. This directly supports the operational realities of Phase 3 and beyond.
